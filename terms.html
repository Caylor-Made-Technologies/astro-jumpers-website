4. Weather

Inflatables can't be used in high winds (about 15–20 mph or more), rain, or lightning. If unsafe weather is expected, we'll work with you to reschedule or refund your deposit. If weather turns unsafe during your rental, everyone must exit the inflatable right away and it must be turned off.

5. Delivery area and setup

  • We deliver to Hereford, TX and surrounding towns out to Amarillo, Olton, and Hart. [PLACEHOLDER] Delivery fees outside Hereford may apply.
  • You'll need a flat, clear area a little larger than the rental, free of rocks, sharp objects, sprinkler heads, and low branches or power lines.
  • Grass setups are staked; concrete or driveway setups use sandbags. We can't set up on gravel or steep slopes.
  • A standard outdoor outlet within about 50 feet, on its own circuit, is required (or ask about a generator).
  • Please give us a clear path from the street to the setup spot. Our crew may decline a setup that isn't safe.

6. Safety rules

  • A responsible adult (18+) must watch the inflatable at all times while it's in use.
  • Follow the posted limits for number of riders and sizes. Keep similar-size kids together.
  • No shoes, glasses, sharp objects, food, drinks, gum, silly string, face paint, or pets inside.
  • No flips, wrestling, or climbing on the walls.
  • Keep the blower running and its cord clear. Don't move the inflatable once it's set up.
