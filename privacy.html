What we collect

When you book a rental, we collect what you type into the booking form: your name, phone number, email, event address, town, event date, the rental you chose, and any notes. We also record when you agreed to our Rental Terms.

We don't ask for payment card numbers on this website.

How we use it

  • To confirm, deliver, set up, and pick up your rental.
  • To contact you about your booking by phone, text, or email, including a "booking received" email and a "confirmed" email.
  • To keep basic records of our rentals.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only the Astro Jumpers owner can see booking details. Other website visitors can only see which rentals are taken on which dates, never names or contact details.

We use a few service providers to run the website, and they handle data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: stores bookings securely.
  • Our email provider [PLACEHOLDER: Brevo or Resend]: sends booking emails.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts: provides the website's fonts (your browser requests them from Google).

How long we keep it

[PLACEHOLDER] We keep booking records for up to 3 years for our business records, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, unless we need to keep it for an open booking or a legal reason.

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. If you use the owner's admin page, your browser stores a login session.

Children

Our rentals are for kids, but bookings must be made by a parent or another adult (18+). We don't knowingly collect information from children.
